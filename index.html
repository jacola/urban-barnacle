    Within path uses the per-path Top 250 pages (Warriors, Rogues, Mages, Poets); a player's path is only known once they have appeared on one of them. Real rank adds the players who are missing from today's lists (unregistered) but whose last-known power is higher. Players with hidden stats are bounded by their nearest visible neighbours, which can leave an uncertain range. Power to next compares against the nearest better-ranked player whose character page shows stats.

    Rank history, real rank and power to next are available for every player who has appeared on the list since archiving began. Data: players.json, history.json (detailed daily record for the config.json characters), snapshots in data/snapshots/.
