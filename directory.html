Player power list

Official rankings and all known players in one table. Click a column to sort; use the checkbox menus to combine filters. Observed power, rank bounds and mark minimums are labeled separately. View chart.

Power by rank

Only ranked players with visible stats can appear on a rank chart. Search and filters above also apply here.

Power is vita + 2 × mana. A number is an observed value as of its check date; a range is bounded by neighbours in an official ranking; ≥ is a mark-based minimum. Values from earlier checks say “last known.” Estimates do not assign an unofficial rank. “To next” applies only when the player and the next better-ranked player have visible power.

Activity from official clan and subpath lists: active means played within 15 days, inactive means 15–30 days, and absent means 30+ days. A blank field means the sources do not establish it. Path icons from Nexus Atlas; mark and subpath icons provided by the site owner.
